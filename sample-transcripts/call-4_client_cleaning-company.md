# Call 4: client upgrade call, cleaning company (FICTIONAL)

**FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Every person, business and product here is invented for practice. Kettlebook is a made-up booking, reminder and invoicing app for small appointment businesses.

Speakers: **Lee** (account manager, Kettlebook). **Femi** (client, owns a cleaning company with six cleaners, on the basic Kettlebook plan). Call type: existing client asked for a call.

---

**Lee:** Hi Femi, you asked for a call?

**Femi:** Yes. Twice last month two of my cleaners turned up at the same house and nobody turned up at the other one. One client nearly left us. We need the team scheduling add-on. My ops manager tested it on the trial and it does what we need. Just tell me how to turn it on, and can you start it from the first of the month?

**Lee:** Yes to both. I'll set it up today and it bills from the first.

**Femi:** Good. While I have you: reminders have been brilliant, no complaints there.

**Lee:** Glad to hear it. We hear chasing payments is the number one pain in cleaning. How's that for you?

**Femi:** Honestly, bad. Commercial clients pay on thirty-day terms and stretch it to sixty. My accountant says I should be using automatic payment reminders. I just haven't set anything up.

**Lee:** OK, noted. Anything else?

**Femi:** One worry. The team add-on puts your app on the cleaners' phones. Two of my older staff don't trust apps much. I'm not sure your app is simple enough for them.

**Lee:** We can do a short training call with them.

**Femi:** Maybe. Let's see.
