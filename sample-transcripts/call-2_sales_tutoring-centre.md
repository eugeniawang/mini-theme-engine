# Call 2: follow-up sales call, tutoring centre (FICTIONAL)

**FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Every person, business and product here is invented for practice. Kettlebook is a made-up booking, reminder and invoicing app for small appointment businesses. SlotPilot is also made up.

Speakers: **Sam** (seller, Kettlebook). **Tom** (buyer, runs a tutoring centre with four tutors). Call type: second sales call, after a recorded demo.

---

**Sam:** Hi Tom. Did you get a chance to watch the demo?

**Tom:** I did, twice. It looks good. Can I be straight with you about two things?

**Sam:** Please.

**Tom:** First, the parents. Most of them are used to calling me. Will they actually use your booking link, or will they still just ring me and I'll end up typing it in myself?

**Sam:** Most centres see about two-thirds of parents switch in the first month.

**Tom:** Maybe. My parents are not most parents. Second thing: you're forty-nine a month. I trialled SlotPilot last spring at twelve a month. Is yours really worth four times as much?

**Sam:** SlotPilot doesn't take deposits, and that's where most of the value is for a centre like yours.

**Tom:** Right, and that's the bit I care about. The no-shows are the main reason I'm here. Last term I lost a tutor's whole Thursday to kids who didn't come. The deposit feature you showed in the demo is exactly what I need. I'm in. What does the contract look like?

**Sam:** Month to month, no lock-in. I can send it over today.

**Tom:** Send it. I'll read it tonight.

**Sam:** Will do. Anything else?

**Tom:** No, the coffee here is terrible but that's not your problem.
