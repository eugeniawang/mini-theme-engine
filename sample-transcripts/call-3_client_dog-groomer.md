# Call 3: client check-in, mobile dog groomer (FICTIONAL)

**FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Every person, business and product here is invented for practice. Kettlebook is a made-up booking, reminder and invoicing app for small appointment businesses.

Speakers: **Lee** (account manager, Kettlebook). **Ines** (client, mobile dog groomer, has used Kettlebook booking and reminders for eight months). Call type: quarterly check-in with an existing client.

---

**Lee:** Hi Ines, quick check-in. How's Kettlebook working for you?

**Ines:** Booking is great. The reminders have cut the no-shows a lot.

**Lee:** Good to hear. Anything still bugging you?

**Ines:** Getting paid is still the worst bit. People say they'll transfer the money and then three weeks later I'm sending awkward messages. I don't know what else I can do about it.

**Lee:** We have an invoicing add-on that sends a payment link with the booking and chases it for you.

**Ines:** Oh, I didn't know you did that.

**Lee:** I'll send you the details. Anything else?

**Ines:** Reminders help, but I still get people cancelling the morning of. I read that some groomers charge a late-cancellation fee. I've been thinking about doing that.

**Lee:** That makes sense. How's the rest of the week looking?

**Ines:** Full. I drive about two hours a day between clients, but that's just the job with mobile grooming.

**Lee:** Two hours a day, wow.

**Ines:** You get used to it. Good podcasts.

**Lee:** Ha. I'll email the invoicing info today.
