# Call 1: sales discovery call, hair studio (FICTIONAL)

**FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Every person, business and product here is invented for practice. Kettlebook is a made-up booking, reminder and invoicing app for small appointment businesses.

Speakers: **Sam** (seller, Kettlebook). **Dana** (buyer, owns a two-chair hair studio). Call type: first sales call.

---

**Sam:** Thanks for making time, Dana. How's the studio going?

**Dana:** Busy, which is good. Two chairs, me and one other stylist, open five days.

**Sam:** Great. A lot of salon owners tell us chasing deposits is their biggest headache. Is that true for you?

**Dana:** Not really, I don't take deposits. Never have.

**Sam:** Fair enough. So what made you book this call?

**Dana:** Saturdays, mostly. I'll have two or three people just not turn up. That's a whole chair sitting empty. I've been looking at those apps that text people a reminder the day before. There's like four of them and I haven't picked one.

**Sam:** How do people book with you now?

**Dana:** Text, Instagram messages, sometimes a phone call. The back-and-forth to find a time is killing me. I'm answering texts at eleven at night. I don't even know what the fix for that is, honestly.

**Sam:** And the admin side, invoices, books?

**Dana:** Oh, I do all my books on Sunday nights. That's just part of owning a business, it's fine. Takes maybe four hours.

**Sam:** Four hours every Sunday?

**Dana:** About that. Anyway, the empty chairs are the thing.

**Sam:** Understood. Let me show you how Kettlebook handles reminders, and we can come back to booking.

**Dana:** Sounds good.
